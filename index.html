How to play

One simple rule. Plenty of ways to get it wrong.

1

Drag a ring

Turn any ring and every bead on it slides along. It snaps into place, one turn at a time.

2

Watch the groups

Beads sit where rings cross, so every turn helps one group and disturbs another. Plan ahead.

3

Make each group one color

Solve it in par turns or fewer for three stars. Undo and restart are always free.
