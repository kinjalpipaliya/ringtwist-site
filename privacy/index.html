Ringtwist Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Ringtwist: IQ Logic Puzzle ("Ringtwist", "the app") is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android made by Kinjal Pipaliya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your progress stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional in-app purchases through Apple or Google, with RevenueCat keeping track of what you bought. Those services receive some device information, described below.

Information kept only on your phone

  • Game progress and settings: levels cleared, stars, diamonds, and your sound, haptics and colorblind settings.
  • Play data: a record of game events such as starting or clearing a level, using a hint, watching an ad or making a purchase. It holds only game facts (level, turns, time, balance), nothing that identifies you. You can view it in Settings > Play data.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We do not use an analytics service. You can clear your progress in Settings > Reset progress, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: ads between levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for diamonds. To choose and deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google receives information from your device, such as its IP address, device type, operating system, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with the ads.

  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you allow it does Google receive your device's advertising identifier and show ads based on your interests. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised, and the game plays exactly the same.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form when you first open it, where you can accept, reject or choose which purposes to allow.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Buying "Remove ads" stops the ads between levels. Optional ads you choose to watch for diamonds stay available.

Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment, and we never see your payment details.

To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase history for Ringtwist, and basic device information such as device type, operating system, country and app version. It does not receive your name or email from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The app does not use your microphone or camera.

Children

Ringtwist is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your tracking choice at any time on iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Reset your progress in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, requests about ad data are best made to Google, and about purchase records to RevenueCat, Apple or Google. We are happy to help: contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
